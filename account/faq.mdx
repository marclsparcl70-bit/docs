Have a question about Music Events? Browse the most common questions below. If you don't find the answer you need, our support team is available at [support@musicevents.com](mailto:support@musicevents.com).

## Attending Events

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I buy tickets?">
    Go to any event page and click **Get Tickets**. Select your ticket type and quantity, log in or create an account, and complete payment. Your tickets are emailed to you and saved under **My Tickets**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Music Events accepts all major credit and debit cards — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and Discover — as well as Apple Pay and Google Pay on supported devices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund on my ticket?">
    Refund policies are set by the event host and are shown on the event page before you purchase. If an event is cancelled, you will automatically receive a full refund within 5–10 business days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I access my tickets on the day of the event?">
    Open the Music Events app or website and go to **My Tickets**. Show your QR code to staff at the door. Download your ticket in advance if you expect poor connectivity at the venue.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?">
    Yes. Go to **My Tickets**, select the ticket you want to transfer, and click **Transfer**. Enter your recipient's email address and confirm. The ticket moves to their account immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if an event is cancelled or postponed?">
    If an event is cancelled, all ticketed attendees are automatically notified and refunded. For postponements, you will receive an email with the new date and the option to keep your ticket or request a refund.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Hosting Events

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create an event?">
    Switch to a Host account in your profile settings, then go to **Dashboard > Create Event**. Fill in your event details, configure ticketing, and click **Publish**. See the [Create Event](/hosting/create-event) guide for full step-by-step instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I receive payouts from ticket sales?">
    Connect a bank account in **Dashboard > Settings > Payouts**. Funds are deposited 2–3 business days after your event ends, minus the platform fee.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the fees for hosting an event?">
    Music Events charges a small service fee per ticket sold. Free events (RSVP only) have no fees. See the Pricing page on musicevents.com for current rates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I edit my event after publishing?">
    Yes. Go to **Dashboard > My Events**, select your event, and click **Edit**. You can update most details at any time. Attendees are notified automatically if the date, time, or venue changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I check in attendees at the door?">
    Use the Music Events mobile app or the Dashboard check-in view to scan attendee QR codes. Both tools work offline and sync automatically when you reconnect.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
