Music Events sends you notifications to keep you informed about events you care about. You have full control over which notifications you receive and how — choose between email, push notifications, or both.

## Notification Types

Music Events supports the following notification types:

- **Event reminders** — Sent 24 hours and 1 hour before events you've ticketed or RSVP'd to.
- **New event announcements** — Alerts when artists and venues you follow publish new events.
- **Ticket purchase confirmations** — Immediate confirmation whenever you complete a ticket purchase.
- **Event updates** — Notifications about changes to date, time, or venue for events you're attending.
- **Promotional emails and featured event picks** — Curated highlights and special offers from Music Events.
- **Host notifications** — Updates on ticket sales and new attendees. Available for Host accounts only.

## Managing Notification Preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your profile icon and go to **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Notifications">
    Select **Notifications** from the settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle notification types">
    Turn each notification type on or off according to your preference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your delivery method">
    Select your preferred delivery method for each notification: **Email**, **Push Notification**, or **Both**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your preferences">
    Click **Save Preferences** to apply your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep event reminders enabled so you never miss a show you've paid for.
</Tip>

## Unsubscribing from Emails

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by clicking the **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom of any email. Transactional emails — such as ticket confirmations and password resets — cannot be disabled.

<Note>
  Unsubscribing from marketing emails does not affect event reminders or important account notifications.
</Note>

## Push Notifications on Mobile

Enable push notifications in the Music Events mobile app by allowing notifications when prompted on first launch. You can update this permission at any time in your phone's system settings under **Apps > Music Events > Notifications**.
