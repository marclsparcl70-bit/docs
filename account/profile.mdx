Your Music Events profile controls how others see you and helps the platform personalize your event recommendations. Keep it up to date with your city and preferred genres to get the most relevant suggestions.

## Editing Your Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile menu">
    Click your profile picture in the top-right corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter edit mode">
    Select **Edit Profile** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Update your display name, profile photo, bio, and location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save Changes** to apply your updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Add your favorite genres to receive better event recommendations in your feed.
</Tip>

## Switching Account Type

You can switch between an Attendee account and a Host account at any time. Go to **Profile Settings > Account Type** and select your preferred mode.

<Note>
  Switching to Host mode unlocks the event creation dashboard. Your ticket purchase history and saved events are preserved.
</Note>

## Changing Your Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Security settings">
    Go to **Profile Settings > Security**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the password change">
    Click **Change Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your passwords">
    Enter your current password, then type your new password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your new password">
    Click **Save** to confirm the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you forget your password, use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page to reset it via email.
</Note>

## Deleting Your Account

<Warning>
  Deleting your account permanently removes all your data, including tickets, RSVPs, and event history. This action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

To delete your account, go to **Profile Settings > Account > Delete Account**. You'll be asked to confirm via email before deletion is processed.
