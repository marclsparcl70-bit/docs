Music Events helps you find live music that matches your taste. Search across thousands of events by location, genre, artist, or venue — and get personalized suggestions based on your listening history and past attendance.

## Search and Filters

The search bar at the top of the events page lets you search by artist name, venue, city, or keyword. As you type, results update in real time so you can quickly zero in on what you're looking for. Narrow your results further using the available filters:

- **Location** — city or zip code
- **Genre** — rock, jazz, electronic, hip-hop, classical, and more
- **Date range** — browse upcoming events within any window of time
- **Price range** — free, under $25, $25–$50, $50+
- **Event type** — concert, festival, club night, open mic

## Personalized Recommendations

After you follow artists or attend a few events, Music Events surfaces recommendations tailored to your taste directly on your home feed. The more you engage — by saving events, RSVPing, and purchasing tickets — the more accurate your recommendations become. Check the **For You** tab on the events page for a curated list of shows you're likely to enjoy.

## Following Artists and Venues

Stay up to date with the acts and spaces you love by following them directly on the platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an artist or venue page">
    Navigate to the page of any artist or venue you want to follow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Follow'">
    Hit the **Follow** button near the top of their page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See their events in your feed">
    Their upcoming events will appear in your home feed, and you'll receive notifications whenever they announce new shows (if notifications are enabled).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can manage who you follow from your profile under **"Following"**.
</Tip>

## Saving Events

Tap the bookmark icon on any event card to save it to your Saved Events list. This is a great way to keep track of shows you're considering without committing to a purchase or RSVP just yet. Access your saved events at any time from your profile menu.
