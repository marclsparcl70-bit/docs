Many events on Music Events are free or open-door and let you RSVP to save your spot. RSVPing is instant and free — it signals to the host that you're coming and unlocks event reminders so you never miss a show.

## How to RSVP

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event page">
    Navigate to the event you want to attend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'RSVP' or 'I'm Going'">
    Hit the **RSVP** or **I'm Going** button on the event page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your attendance">
    Review the event details and confirm your RSVP when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your confirmation">
    You'll receive a confirmation email and the event is automatically added to your calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Some free events have limited capacity — RSVP early to guarantee your spot.
</Tip>

## Managing Your RSVPs

View all your upcoming RSVPs under **My Events** in your profile. If your plans change, you can cancel an RSVP at any time before the event by clicking **Cancel RSVP** on the event page or directly from your profile. Cancelling frees up your spot for other attendees.

## RSVP vs. Tickets

Not sure which applies to your event? Here's a quick breakdown:

<Info>
  **RSVP** is for free events. No payment is required — your spot is reserved instantly and you can cancel at any time.
</Info>

<Info>
  **Tickets** are for paid events. Payment is required to complete your purchase, a ticket is issued to your account, and cancellations are subject to the event host's refund policy.
</Info>

## Event Reminders

When you RSVP or purchase tickets, Music Events automatically sends you reminder notifications **24 hours** and **1 hour** before the event so you're always prepared. You can customize which reminders you receive and how you get them in your [Notification Settings](/account/notifications).
