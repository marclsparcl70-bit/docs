Music Events makes it easy to purchase tickets for any listed show. Tickets are delivered digitally and accessible directly from your account — no printing required.

## Purchasing Tickets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event page and click 'Get Tickets'">
    Navigate to the event you want to attend and click the **Get Tickets** button on the event page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select ticket type and quantity">
    Choose the ticket tier that suits you and set the number of tickets you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or create an account">
    If you're not already signed in, you'll be prompted to log in or create a free account before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter payment details and confirm your order">
    Provide your payment information and review your order summary, then confirm the purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive and access your tickets">
    Your tickets are emailed to you immediately and saved to your account under **My Tickets**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ticket Types

Events on Music Events offer a variety of ticket categories depending on what the host makes available:

- **General Admission** — standing room access to the event space
- **Reserved Seating** — an assigned seat in a designated section
- **VIP** — premium access, often including perks such as early entry, dedicated viewing areas, or meet-and-greets
- **Early Bird** — a discounted rate available in limited quantities before general sale

## Accessing Your Tickets

After purchase, find your tickets in **My Tickets** under your profile. Each ticket includes a unique QR code that event staff will scan at the door — no physical ticket or printout needed.

<Note>
  Download your tickets before the event in case you have limited connectivity at the venue.
</Note>

## Transferring Tickets

You can transfer a ticket to a friend directly from the **My Tickets** page. Select the ticket you want to transfer, enter the recipient's email address, and confirm the transfer. The recipient receives a notification and the ticket moves to their account automatically. Once transferred, the ticket is removed from your account.

## Refunds and Exchanges

Refund policies are set by the individual event host, so be sure to check the event page for specific terms before purchasing. If an event is cancelled by the host, refunds are automatically processed to your original payment method within 5–10 business days — no action is required on your part.

<Warning>
  Tickets purchased outside the Music Events platform cannot be verified or refunded through our system.
</Warning>
