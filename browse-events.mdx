Music Events gives you access to thousands of upcoming concerts, festivals, and live performances all in one place. Whether you know exactly who you want to see or you're open to discovering something new, you can filter by city, genre, artist name, venue, or date range to zero in on the perfect show.

## Searching for Events

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Events page">
    Navigate to the Events page from the main navigation bar at the top of the site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search by artist, genre, or venue">
    Use the search bar to type an artist name, genre (e.g. "jazz" or "indie rock"), or venue name. Results update as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters to narrow your results">
    Use the filter panel to refine results by location, date range, genre, and price range. You can combine multiple filters at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click an event to view full details">
    Select any event card to open its detail page, where you'll find the full lineup, venue information, ticket options, and more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding Event Listings

Each event card gives you the key information you need at a glance:

- **Event name** — the title of the show or festival
- **Date and time** — the scheduled start time in the venue's local timezone
- **Venue name and address** — where the event is taking place
- **Ticket price range** — the lowest and highest available ticket prices
- **RSVP / ticket availability** — whether tickets are still available or the event is sold out

## Featured and Trending Events

The homepage highlights featured events hand-picked by the Music Events team, trending shows gaining popularity in your area, and editor picks across a range of genres. The more you use the platform, the more tailored your feed becomes based on your listening history and location.

## Following Artists and Venues

Stay in the loop with the artists and venues you love by following them directly on Music Events. When you follow an artist or venue, their upcoming events appear at the top of your feed as soon as they're announced — so you'll never miss a ticket sale or a last-minute show.

To follow an artist or venue:

1. Open any event listing and click the artist or venue name to visit their profile page.
2. Click the **Follow** button on their profile.
3. New events from that artist or venue will appear in your **Following** feed, accessible from the main navigation.

You can manage everything you follow from the [Notifications](/account/notifications) section of your account settings.

<Tip>
  Enable location access in your browser to automatically surface events near you, and follow your favorite artists to get notified the moment new shows are announced.
</Tip>
