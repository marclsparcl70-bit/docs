Creating a Music Events account is free and takes under a minute. With an account you can purchase and store tickets, RSVP to events, follow artists and venues, and receive personalized recommendations based on your taste. If you're a host, you can also create and manage your own events — all from the same account.

## Sign Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit musicevents.com and click 'Sign Up'">
    Head to [musicevents.com](https://musicevents.com) and click the **Sign Up** button in the top-right corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Provide your name, email address, and a password. Your password must be at least 8 characters long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Music Events and click the confirmation link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Set your city and choose your favorite genres so Music Events can start surfacing relevant shows and recommendations right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Attendee vs. Host Accounts

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Attendee">
    Attendee accounts are for people who want to discover events, buy tickets, and RSVP to shows. Once you're signed in, you can follow artists and venues to stay up to date on their upcoming events, and receive personalized recommendations tailored to your location and musical taste.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Event Host">
    Host accounts give you access to event creation tools, ticketing management, and audience analytics. You can publish events, set ticket tiers and pricing, and track attendance — all from your host dashboard. You can switch to a Host account at any time from your profile settings without creating a new account.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Logging In

Sign in at [musicevents.com/login](https://musicevents.com/login) with the email address and password you registered with. If you ever forget your password, click **Forgot password?** on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email to reset it.

<Note>
  Already have an account? Head to [Browse Events](/browse-events) to find your next show.
</Note>
