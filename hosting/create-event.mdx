Music Events gives you everything you need to publish and promote your show. Whether you're hosting a free open mic, a ticketed concert, or a multi-day festival, you can create and manage your event entirely from your dashboard.

## Before You Start

<Note>
  You need a Host account to create events. If you signed up as an attendee, switch to a Host account in your profile settings.
</Note>

Make sure you have the following details ready before creating your event: venue name and address, date and time, event description and artwork, ticket pricing (if applicable), and maximum capacity.

## Creating Your Event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your Dashboard">
    Click your profile icon and select **Dashboard**, then click **Create Event**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add event details">
    Enter the event name, date, time, and venue. Write a description that tells attendees what to expect — genre, artists performing, and any special details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload artwork">
    Add a banner image (recommended: 1200×628px, JPG or PNG). This appears in search results and on the event page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set ticket options">
    Choose **Free** (RSVP only) or **Paid**. For paid events, set your ticket tiers, prices, and quantities. See the Tickets section for pricing guidance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set capacity">
    Enter the maximum number of attendees. Ticket sales stop automatically when capacity is reached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and publish">
    Review your event page, then click **Publish**. Your event is immediately searchable on Music Events.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Event Artwork Guidelines

- Minimum 1200×628px, JPG or PNG format
- Max file size 5MB
- Avoid text-heavy images (title and date are shown automatically)
- Bright and high-contrast images perform best in search

<Tip>
  Events with complete details and high-quality artwork get 3× more views in search results.
</Tip>
