Once your event is published, your host dashboard gives you full control over every aspect of it — from editing details and adjusting ticket inventory to viewing your guest list and checking in attendees on the day of the show.

## Editing Event Details

Go to **Dashboard > My Events** and click on your event, then click **Edit**. You can update the event name, description, date, time, venue, and artwork at any time before the event.

<Warning>
  If you change the date or time, all registered attendees will be notified automatically by email.
</Warning>

## Managing Tickets

From the event editor, go to the **Tickets** tab. You can add new ticket tiers, adjust prices, change quantities, or pause sales on any tier. Note that total capacity cannot be increased beyond your venue's limit once tickets are sold.

## Viewing Your Guest List

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event in your Dashboard">
    Go to **Dashboard > My Events** and click on your event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Attendees'">
    Select the **Attendees** tab on your event management page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your guest list">
    See a full list of ticket holders and RSVP'd guests with their names and ticket types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export for your records">
    Click **Export CSV** to download the full guest list for your records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking In Attendees

On the day of your event, use the Music Events mobile app or the Dashboard check-in view to scan attendees' QR codes. Tickets are marked as used in real time.

<Tip>
  Assign a team member as a door manager — they can access the check-in view without full dashboard access.
</Tip>

## Cancelling an Event

To cancel an event, go to **Dashboard > My Events**, click the event, then click **Cancel Event**. All ticketed attendees are automatically refunded and notified by email.

<Warning>
  Cancelled events cannot be reinstated. Create a new event if you need to reschedule.
</Warning>
