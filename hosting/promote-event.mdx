Getting the word out is just as important as creating a great show. Music Events offers built-in promotion tools to help you reach local music fans, share across social media, and boost visibility in search.

## Sharing Your Event

Every event has a unique public URL you can share anywhere. From the event page, click **Share** to copy the link or post directly to Instagram, Facebook, or X (Twitter).

<Tip>
  Share your event at least 2 weeks before the show to give fans time to plan.
</Tip>

## Featured Listings

Boost your event's visibility by applying for a featured listing. Featured events appear at the top of search results and on the Music Events homepage. Apply from **Dashboard > My Events > Promote**.

<Note>
  Featured listings are reviewed by our curation team and are subject to availability.
</Note>

## Email Your Followers

If you have followers on Music Events, you can send them an event announcement email directly from your dashboard. Go to **Dashboard > My Events > Promote > Announce to Followers**. Your followers receive a single notification email about the event.

## Embedding Your Event

Add a ticket widget to your own website using the embed code. Go to **Dashboard > My Events > Promote > Embed Ticket Widget**. Copy the HTML snippet and paste it into your site. The widget shows ticket availability and links directly to checkout.

```html event-widget.html
<iframe
  src="https://musicevents.com/embed/EVENT_ID"
  width="100%"
  height="400"
  frameborder="0"
  allow="payment"
></iframe>
```

## Tracking Performance

View real-time analytics from **Dashboard > My Events > Analytics**: page views, ticket sales over time, traffic sources, and attendee demographics. Use this data to improve your promotion strategy for future events.
