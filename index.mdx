## Get started in minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up for free at musicevents.com. Choose whether you're joining as an attendee or an event host.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find or create an event">
    Search events by city, genre, or artist — or create your own event and set up ticketing in minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enjoy the music">
    Grab your tickets, get directions, and show up ready to enjoy the show.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Looking to host events? Check out the [Hosting Events](/hosting/create-event) section to set up your first event.
</Note>
